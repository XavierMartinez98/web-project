Welcome to Xavier Martinez's Audio Project!

Description: The place I decided to depict in my audio project was that of an office, with some sort of mechanical door. With an unknown robot roaming around the building trying to get to you, it's late at night and your trying to escape, so you keep it at bay and when it's distracted, run right out. I used 11 sounds all from open game art to make my project. With only simple changes like volume since the audio itself did a lot for hat it was.
