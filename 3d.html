Welcome to Xavier Martinez's 3D Project!

The concept behind this design was based on one of the first ever designs we made in class without a tutorial. After that i thought it would be a really good idea to expand upon with more knowledge to make it look better, since back then it looked very choppy and not very good looking. So i decided to make a simple render, but it could be used as a nice 3d printed statue or something like that if i tinkered with it more, i used most if not all of the same shapes used to make the original, Squares and Cylinders, but i also used a Pyramid and the Scribble tool for the feet of the tub and the water respectively. Sizing and combining was easy, just making them fit and using the group tool to merge it all together, aligning it based on my own measurements.
